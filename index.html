The approach

Supportive, nonjudgmental, and built around your season of healing.

Therapy here is client-centered and solution-focused. You set the pace and the priorities; we work on what actually matters to you, using approaches chosen to fit your situation rather than a fixed program.

  • Anxiety
  • Depression
  • Trauma & EMDR
  • Bipolar symptoms
  • Addiction
  • Relationships & family
  • Stress management
  • Life transitions

What's offered

Four ways to work together

Individual therapy

CBT, DBT, Motivational Interviewing, and EMDR — matched to what you're working through.

Couples therapy

Cognitive Behavioral and strengths-based work for partners who want to communicate differently.

Group therapy

Shared, guided sessions with people working through similar challenges.

Clinical supervision

Supervision and consulting for social workers pursuing licensure and for practicing clinicians.

Who this is for

Individuals and couples at any stage — early adulthood through later transitions.

Get to know us

Hi, I'm Sheena.

I hold a Master's degree in Social Work from Louisiana State University and an undergraduate Social Work degree from Northwestern State University. I'm a Licensed Clinical Social Worker with a training certificate in EMDR therapy.

I've spent years working with depression, anxiety, symptoms of bipolar disorder, addiction, and trauma. I understand the overwhelming challenges we face at different stages of life, and I offer a supportive, nonjudgmental approach shaped around your needs.

I would be honored to be part of your healing journey.
